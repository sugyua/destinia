Destinia iOS ARM32 milestone 0.3.4
Branch: ios/unicorn-poc
Experiment verified in macOS ARM64 CI #42156713768:
- 360 out of 360 rendered RGB565 frame hashes identical in baseline,
  native accelerated graphics, and native graphics with host-backed ARM heap.
- Native-mapped Unicorn heap: 64 MiB, fall back to ordinary map if device
  disallows host-pointer mapping; no altered original Android game assets.
- Actual native drawing operations validated: 86411 filled rows,
  150720 sprite columns across 360 frames.
- Avoid repeated guest-memory RPC and per-column dynamic allocations.
- On-device performance HUD: three-finger tap toggles FPS, ARM32 execution
  average milliseconds and RGB565 preparation milliseconds.
- HUD hidden by default, console metrics every ~2 seconds.
- ARM32 logic rate unchanged; no fake 60-FPS promise.
- CI checked original Android engine 1.0.6; PC 0.17.4 mods not ported.
