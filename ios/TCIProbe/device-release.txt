0.3.8 changes:
- Keep all validated native graphics acceleration of 0.3.7.
- Enable native _Grp_pxlMultiply color-component multiplication
  (DESTINIA_NATIVE_PXLMULTIPLY=1), replacing only the Thumb body in
  Unicorn guest RAM with a version-checked native RGB565 equivalent.
- Preserve the original Android 1.0.6 library and all original assets.
- Keep native diagnostic HUD, three-finger toggle and four-finger profiler.
- Keep game pacing unmodified; target remains 16 logical frames/s (62.5ms).
- Keep original bundle identifier and in-place update compatibility.
